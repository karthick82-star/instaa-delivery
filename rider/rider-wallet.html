Case i: No Damage

Customer inspects groceries and shares 4-digit Delivery OTP. Once entered into the app, your delivery fee (₹40 local / ₹70 inter-town) is immediately credited to your virtual wallet.

If a customer rejects damaged goods, delivery pay is withheld by Admin while you return items to the store. Hand items to the shop manager, enter their 6-digit Shop Return OTP, and your pay is instantly released!
